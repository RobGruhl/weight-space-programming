02 / The patch

Reversal,
not erasure.

The published p50c edit touches selected columns in two tensors. Its main directional component is approximately rank one, but its strength matters.

W′ = W − α u(uᵀW)Applied only to the selected columns; α = 6 in the published patch.

Along the selected direction, the new component is approximately (1 − α) times the original. At α = 1 it disappears. At α = 6 it becomes −5 times its original value: reversed and amplified.

Illustration, not model inference

Turn the edit strength

The selected component becomes −5× its original value: reversed and amplified.

This is one-dimensional arithmetic, not a simulation of model outputs. Other components are approximately preserved; rounding and input-dependent activations still matter.

Quantized rounding gives the realized deltas a formal numerical rank greater than one. That does not mean there are many substantial mechanisms: one component accounts for nearly all their energy.

What this does not establish

The audit does not prove that the selected direction means repetition, or that an observed benefit is specific to repetition. Those are causal and semantic questions, not consequences of a singular-value decomposition.

Why shuffle the direction?

The behavioral control perturbs the same columns with a similarly sized but shuffled delta. It asks whether the original direction matters more than a nearby generic perturbation. A harmful shuffled edit is not decisive evidence for the original mechanism: it could simply damage something else.

A reverse-sign intervention was proposed in the geometry analysis, but was not run. It is not a fourth arm in the results.
