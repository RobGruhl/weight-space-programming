04 / Weights-only reading

A prediction that did not survive the probe.

Could weight-derived signals rank which attention heads look one token back? The frozen combined ranking failed.

−0.056Spearman rank correlation
48Heads across six attention layers
20 × 64Synthetic sequences × tokens

The question was specific

The reader used weights and architecture to construct proxy positional profiles, then froze a score for every full-attention head. The probe later measured mean attention probability assigned to the previous token, over query positions 16–63.

The reader knew the synthetic sequences and wrote the English passages. What remained unseen was the model’s actual attention on those sequences. “Blind” here describes the outcomes, not the inputs.

Why this failure is informative

High-frequency rotary energy looked like a plausible clue for adjacent-token attention. In this measurement it pointed the wrong way. Other weight-derived components had modest descriptive correlations, but those observations do not rescue the failed combined prediction.

Forty-one of forty-eight heads favored offset one over other offsets from 1–47, yet with small attention mass. A weak common local tilt is different from strong specialized previous-token heads.

Do not generalize the negative result too far.

This evaluates one proxy on one synthetic set in one model. It is not a disproof of weight reading, and it does not evaluate the original publication’s editor, models or tasks. Attention heads share layers and key-value groups; the permutation test is a diagnostic null, not 48 independent replications.
